How to clean a stock CSV for a Kenyan small business

A shop, distributor, or small team might receive a stock list from a till, supplier, marketplace, or spreadsheet. Before importing it elsewhere, make the column headings and surrounding spaces consistent. This walkthrough uses invented data and shows what basic AutoOps Hub CSV cleanup does.

Try the free CSV allowance ↗ Download sample CSV

1. Start with a safe sample

Download the fictional stock list or create a copy of your own file. Keep the original untouched so you can compare results. The sample has invented product codes, prices, quantities, and counties; it contains no customer information.

Before cleanup

 Product SKU , Product Name , Stock Qty , Unit Price KES , County
 KS-001 , Maize Flour 2kg , 12 , 230 , Nairobi
 KS-002 , Rice 1kg , 8 , 175 , Machakos
 KS-003 , Soap Bar , 24 , 95 , Makueni

Notice the spaces around the headings and values.

2. Run the CSV cleanup

Open AutoOps Hub, create or sign in to your account, choose the sample CSV, and run the CSV cleanup workflow. The free allowance supports up to two jobs, with up to 100 data rows per CSV. No custom domain is required.

Basic cleanup trims headings, uses lowercase, replaces spaces in headings with underscores, and trims whitespace around cell values. It does not decide whether stock counts or prices are correct.

After header and whitespace cleanup

product_sku,product_name,stock_qty,unit_price_kes,county
KS-001,Maize Flour 2kg,12,230,Nairobi
KS-002,Rice 1kg,8,175,Machakos
KS-003,Soap Bar,24,95,Makueni

This is the expected shape for the fictional example, not a claim that anyone's business file has already been processed.

3. Review before using the file

  • Confirm the output headings match what your next tool expects.
  • Compare several rows with the original file.
  • Check stock counts, prices, formulas, and totals separately; this tool does not reconcile inventory or calculate accounting totals.
  • Keep an original copy for comparison.
Privacy reminder: don't upload passwords, M-Pesa or bank details, private server logs, personal customer records, or confidential information unless you have authority and a legitimate reason to process it. A sample file is safest for a first test.

When is this useful?

This first-pass workflow may help when CSV headings use mixed capitalization or spacing, values contain accidental spaces, or a small file needs a repeatable formatting pass before a manual check. For larger jobs or other formats, check plan eligibility in the live app.

Common questions

Does this guarantee spreadsheet accuracy?

No. It formats headers and trims spaces. Validate values, stock counts, and totals separately.

Does Free remove duplicate or blank rows?

No. The free CSV path does not remove duplicate or blank rows. That cleanup is available only on higher plan tiers in the current application logic.

Do I need a custom domain?

No. Use the existing AutoOps Hub Render URL; no domain purchase or DNS change is needed.
